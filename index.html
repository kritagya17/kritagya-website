About Me

Future Doctor.
Present Builder.

I'm Kritagya Raj Pandey — a third-year MBBS student at Maharajgunj Medical Campus, Institute of Medicine, Kathmandu, Nepal. I secured Rank #1 in CEE 2080 across Nepal, but for me, medicine has always been more than an exam score.

My goal: become an exceptional physician and a trusted voice in medical education online.
